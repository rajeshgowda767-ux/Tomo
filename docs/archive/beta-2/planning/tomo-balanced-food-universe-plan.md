# Tomo Balanced Food Universe Plan

Scope: proposal only. No recipe data was changed.

Source: `docs/tomo-food-balance-audit.md`

## Goal

Create a balanced Indian food universe, not just a larger dish count.

This plan prioritizes missing archetypes, underrepresented regions, and underrepresented meal families while avoiding more variants of already-heavy families.

## Do Not Add In This Pass

- More dosa variants
- More paratha variants
- More paneer variants
- More chicken variants

## Missing Archetypes

- Dal / Pappu / Saaru: everyday lentil mains are thin compared with rice.
- Kootu / Palya / Poriyal / Thoran: vegetable sides and light South Indian lunch plates are underbuilt.
- Saag / greens: very low presence across lunch and dinner.
- Kadhi family: present but shallow.
- West Indian lunch: needs more Gujarati and Maharashtrian non-snack meals.
- East Indian lunch: needs more Odia, Bengali, and Assamese everyday staples.
- Northeast staples: still sparse, especially breakfast and snacks.
- Healthy snacks and drinks: snacks lean heavier/fried; drinks and lighter snacks are thin.

## Expected Impact By Meal

### Breakfast

Adds useful coverage without more dosa/paratha variants:

- Dhokla
- Ragi Malt
- Ghugni

Expected impact:

- More West Indian breakfast/snack representation
- More steamed and drink-based breakfast options
- Less dependence on dosa/paratha/egg families

### Lunch

This is the strongest improvement area.

Expected impact:

- More dal, pappu, saaru, kadhi, kootu, saag, sabzi
- Stronger West Indian lunch representation
- Stronger East Indian lunch representation
- More vegetable-forward and lentil-forward meals
- Less rice-family dominance

### Dinner

Expected impact:

- More light home-style dinners
- More greens and vegetable mains
- More dal-based comfort dinners
- Less dependence on curry/soup-heavy dinner recommendations

### Snacks

Expected impact:

- More healthy snacks
- More steamed snacks
- More legume-based snacks
- More drink/soother options
- Better East/Northeast/West snack spread
