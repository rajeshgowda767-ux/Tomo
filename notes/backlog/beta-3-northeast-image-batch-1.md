- Bamboo Shoot Pork: pork pieces, bamboo shoots, northeastern homestyle curry.

- Phagshapa: pork and radish stew, Bhutan/Sikkim style appearance.
- Smoked Pork Curry: visibly smoked pork, rich dark curry.
- Smoked Pork Rice: rice with smoked pork pieces.
- Sticky Rice: Northeast bamboo basket style serving.

- Tripuri Berma Curry: Tripura-style fish/berma curry.
- Tungrymbai: fermented soybean dish, authentic Khasi appearance.
- Wahan Mosdeng: pork salad/chutney style, Meghalaya/Tripura tribal cuisine.
