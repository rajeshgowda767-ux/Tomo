## Top 5 Weaknesses

1. Soy/Tofu coverage is effectively absent, which limits vegetarian protein diversity.
2. East India, Northeast India, Coastal India, and West India are still far behind South/North India despite recent expansion.
3. Several dish families are crowded, especially chicken, rice-bowl, dosa, and paneer-rice, raising repetition risk.
4. Pairing coverage is good but not complete; older dishes without pairings can feel thinner in future pairing UX.
5. Quick Guide coverage is improving but still not universal, so Dish Detail quality will vary by recommendation.
